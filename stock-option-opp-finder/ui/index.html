How to read this

Expectancy
Average profit or loss per trade, scratches included. Positive expectancy with a confidence interval that includes zero is not evidence.
R
Profit divided by the defined risk. Losing the full spread is about −1R before commissions.
Signed drift
Stock return after a signal, flipped for shorts. Positive helps a credit vertical; it is not option profit.
Breach
The stock moved against the credit side by more than the expected move implied at the signal. Frequent breaches mean the premium was not enough for the path.
Unmarked
The historical chain had no quote for the same strikes on the exit day, so the trade was left out rather than priced with a model.
NBIS options
No historical NBIS chain exists in the free data used here, so NBIS has stock patterns only. Any NBIS option trade is untested.
