Words explained
Option
A contract someone buys for protection or a bet. You can sell it to them and keep what they pay.
Spread
Sell one option and buy a cheaper one next to it. The one you buy caps your worst loss.
Put spread
You win if the stock does not fall below your sold price.
Call spread
You win if the stock does not rise above your sold price.
Strike
The price written on the option.
Expiry
The last day the option exists. We close long before it.
Limit price / credit
The least money you accept to open the spread. Your broker will not fill it for less.
Paper trading
Practice trading with fake money in your broker's simulator.
